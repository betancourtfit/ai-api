<objective>
Carve a **pure domain layer** and a **port layer** out of the existing modules, and cut the vendor-SDK dependency out of routing policy. Every module that moves keeps a re-export shim at its old path, so the 111-test suite continues to import exactly what it imports today.

Purpose: closes the audit's dependency-inversion violations (V-03, V-04, V-07) and the state-globals violation (V-05, partially) without touching the HTTP layer at all. Splitting this from the delivery refactor (plan 03) means a red suite in this plan can only mean "a move broke something", never "a route changed".
Output: `domain/` (7 modules), `application/ports/` (5 interfaces), `adapters/outbound/` (3 modules), 6 compatibility shims at the old paths, and the executable boundary guard `tests/architecture/boundaries.test.ts`.
</objective>
